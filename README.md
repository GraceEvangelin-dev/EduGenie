EduGenie – AI-Powered Educational Assistant

EduGenie is an AI-powered educational assistant designed to make learning more personalized, interactive, and effective. It helps students understand concepts, practice through quizzes, explore real-life examples, and organize their studies.

📌 Project Overview

EduGenie aims to enhance the learning experience by combining artificial intelligence with educational tools. It supports students in learning at their own pace, improving their understanding of subjects, and planning their academic activities.

✨ Features

- Personalized Learning: Supports learning experiences tailored to students' needs.
- Interactive Quizzes: Helps students test their knowledge and improve their understanding.
- Real-Life Examples: Connects theoretical concepts with practical applications.
- Study Planning: Helps students organize their study schedules and learning goals.
- AI-Powered Assistance: Provides educational support through AI-based features.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Make learning more accessible and engaging.
- Encourage self-paced and independent learning.
- Improve students' understanding through interactive practice.
- Support effective study planning and academic productivity.
